Noodle Varieties

Three cuts, milled in-house

Our noodles are kneaded with kansui water and rested overnight. Choose your cut — every broth has a favourite.

Thin & Straight

Hakata-style, 1.1 mm. The classic partner for tonkotsu — slurp it before it drinks the broth.

Best with Tonkotsu

Flat & Silky

Wide, ribbon-like strands that carry clear shoyu and miso broth right to the last spoon.

Best with Shoyu

Thick & Chewy

Extra-large cut for mazesoba and tantanmen — hearty bite that stands up to sesame and chilli.

Best with Dry Noodles

Why NoriBowl

Small details, big difference

18-hour broth

Bones simmer overnight, skimmed by hand every hour until the broth turns naturally creamy.

Noodles every morning

Two fresh batches a day, rested overnight for the springy bite ramen deserves.

Market-fresh toppings

Vegetables from the Saturday market, eggs from a farm we have used for a decade.

Family-run since 2014

The Room

Twelve stools, warm timber, low hum of slurping

NoriBowl is built around a single timber counter. Steam fogs the windows, jazz plays soft, and the kitchen is close enough to hear the noodles hit the water. It is loud in the good way.
