The Broth Journal

Eighteen hours, three hands, one pot

  1. 01

    Bones in at 4 a.m.

    Pork femurs are roasted, rinsed and set to a rolling simmer before the sun clears the harbour.

  2. 02

    Skimmed every hour

    Scum and foam come off by hand so the broth stays clean, sweet and ivory-white — never murky.

  3. 03

    Tare, then bowl

    Every bowl is seasoned to order with aged tare, then married with fresh noodles within seconds.

Head Chef

From the Chef

Kenji Nakamura

Kenji learned the trade in Fukuoka's smallest shops — eight seats, one pot, no shortcuts. He opened NoriBowl in 2014 with a single rule taped inside the kitchen: “Make one bowl properly, then make the next one.”

Ten years later the rule is still taped to the wall, and the Saturday queue still starts before we open.
