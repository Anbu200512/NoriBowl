How It Works

From first message to last bowl

  1. 01

    Send an enquiry

    Date, headcount, location and anything we should know. Use the form on the contact page — it takes two minutes.

  2. 02

    We build the menu

    Within a day we send a package suggestion, dietary options and a fixed quote. Adjustments are free until you confirm.

  3. 03

    Confirm 48 hours ahead

    A 25% deposit locks the date. Final headcount is due two days before service, then you can relax.

  4. 04

    We arrive and serve

    Crew lands 60 minutes early, sets up, serves hot and leaves the space exactly as we found it.
